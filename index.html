REMOTE SENSING · URBAN CLIMATE · GEOAI

Understanding urban thermal environments through geospatial intelligence.

我以卫星遥感、无人机与多源时空大数据为手段，研究城市热岛的时空演变机理、局地气候分区（LCZ）框架下的热环境响应，以及城市韧性与活力的量化评估，致力于为可持续城市提供数据驱动的科学依据。
I study surface urban heat islands, local climate zones, and urban resilience with multi-source geospatial big data — toward more sustainable and livable cities.
